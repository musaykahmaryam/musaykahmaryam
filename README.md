## ✦ About Me

Hi, I'm **Musaykah** — an undergraduate **Software Engineering** student at **Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology (SSUET)** in Karachi, Pakistan.

I'm drawn to the part of software that people actually see and touch. I'm exploring **frontend development**, **responsive web design** and **UI/UX**, and I want to build digital experiences that are clean, accessible and genuinely useful.

- 🔭 **Right now:** building strong foundations in web development and version control
- 🧩 **What drives me:** curiosity, solving problems step by step, and understanding *why* things work
- 🌱 **How I grow:** small, consistent progress — learning something new and putting it into practice
- 💬 **Open to:** learning together, beginner-friendly collaborations and honest feedback
